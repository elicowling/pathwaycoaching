Couples Know Honesty™ · Presented by Pathway Coaching

You never agreed on how you talk to each other.

You just fell into it. Habits from your parents, past relationships, learned responses. We call that communication and hope it holds.

Come to a workshop Host a Couples Know Honesty™ night

The real issue

At work, we would never run a team that way.

We write it down. We train it. We spend money on it. The expectations and the SOPs are clear.

At home, most of us wing it. Every couple hits the same list anyway. Money, parenting, intimacy, family, work, what happens next. Underneath nearly all of it sits the same thing.

You have built a life together. It is worth building the way you communicate, too.

Most tools help you survive a difficult conversation when one shows up. This goes underneath that. It changes what the two of you agree to, so you have real conversations all the time instead of difficult conversations sometimes.

The model

Open + Honest = Real Communication

100% honest. 100% open. It is simple. Simple is not the same as easy.

Honesty

Being truly and freely yourself™, speaking into what you want and how you feel.

Openness

Listening without reservation™, putting your needs and wants on pause for the person across from you.

In the room

A workshop, not a lecture.

Nobody sits and listens for two and a half hours. You learn the Know Honesty™ model and then use it right there, together, on one real area of your own life.

This is not counseling, and it is not only for couples in trouble. It is for any couple who wants to take a good relationship further. Newlyweds, thirty years in, and everyone between.

You will leave with

  • The Agreement. A common language for openness and honesty
  • An understanding of what blocks your communication. Fake You, The Wall and The Still
  • One real conversation, already started. The time and the tools to put Know Honesty™ into practice before you leave the room
  • The Know / Want / Do™ framework, and Our Pursuit for the two of you together
  • Clear next steps
